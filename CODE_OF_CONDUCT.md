# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
background or identity. We pledge to act and interact in ways that keep the community open, welcoming,
and respectful.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community.

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer through a private channel (see
`SECURITY.md` for how to reach the maintainer privately). Reports will be reviewed and handled
fairly and confidentially.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
